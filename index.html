Glyz Bunny: Cable Crossing

Help Glyz Bunny connect all the servers! Stretch cables, hop over bugs, collect stars and answer IT questions to stay online.

How to play

  1. Hold to stretch the cable. Release to drop it.
  2. Land on the glowing pink port for a PERFECT. Chain perfects for bigger bonuses!
  3. Bugs guard the cable! Tap / SPACE while hopping to JUMP over crawling bugs. From level 3 they move, and from level 4 flying bugs appear. Don't jump into those, wait for them to pass!
  4. Collect stars for points. Every 8 stars = 1 hint 💡.
  5. Grab the shield bubble to block one bug.
  6. After each connection, answer a question about the server you just connected to (a DNS server asks about DNS!) before time runs out. Use a 💡 hint to remove 2 wrong answers.
  7. 3 correct answers in a row = +1 life (max 3).
  8. Reach the GOAL server (checkered line + flag) to clear the stage! Its question is worth double. Every stage is a new world with new surprises.
  9. Clear all 5 worlds to win the game! Then you can keep going in Endless mode.
